# Workflows

The workflow engine and the 17 seeded workflows (SPEC §11) were built in **Phase 8**. Definitions live in `lib/domain/workflows/catalog.ts` (pure, with a dry-run test per workflow in `catalog.test.ts`); the server side is `lib/workflows/engine.ts`. Admin → Workflows lists them with run counts, the on/off switch, each one's steps, a **Test** (dry run against a real record, optionally "as if it were" another time) and the run log with Retry.

## Engine rules

- Domain events land in `events`. For each event the engine finds the workflows whose trigger matches (event type + trigger conditions) and runs their actions in order.
- **Once per (workflow, event):** the run row (`workflow_runs`, unique `idempotency_key = <workflow>:<event id>`) is inserted before anything is sent; the inline dispatch right after the action and the worker can both try, and the second one stops at the unique index.
- **Waits** ("2 days after", "the day before at 4 pm", the review and referral delays from Messaging settings) park the run as `waiting` with `resume_at`; the worker resumes due runs every minute. **Exit rules** are checked before every step after a wait (for example, estimate follow-ups stop once the estimate is accepted or the customer replied).
- **Quiet hours** (Messaging settings, default 8 pm – 8 am Pacific): an automated text due then waits until they end, unless the customer texted us in the last 24 hours. Emails aren't held.
- **Consent:** Do Not Contact blocks everything; STOP blocks texts; marketing needs opt-in (texts) or no unsubscribe (email). Marketing texts get "Reply STOP to opt out.", marketing email the footer and an unsubscribe link. A blocked send is logged on the run with the reason; `sms_or_email` falls back to the other channel.
- **Templates:** every send uses a template from Admin → Messaging & Templates (all "Draft – owner to approve" until approved). A send with a merge field that has no value is held back and logged.
- **Transactional vs marketing** comes from the template. Every message goes to `integration_outbox` as "Simulated – not sent" and onto the lead's or job's conversation.
- **Turned off:** each event is logged as a `skipped` run; nothing is sent; turning it back on doesn't replay them.
- **Failures:** a failed step stops the run (`failed`, shown on Admin → Workflows); Retry continues from that step.
- **Single source:** customer sends that earlier phases made directly (publish notices, rain-delay text, daily updates, overdue reminders, the on-time credit notice) now happen only through workflows 8, 9, 11, 14 and 10. Stage moves, holds, invoices and attention rules stay built in ("Built in" on each workflow page).

## Money (Phase 7)

| Trigger                                                  | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Payment recorded on the deposit invoice (paid in full)   | `jobs.deposit_paid_at` stamped; onboarding's deposit check passes, the item clears and the **Deposit Hold** is released; the job moves on through the onboarding gate when everything else is cleared. Accounting "would sync" preview in the outbox.                     |
| Owner approves a credit (or an On-Time Guarantee credit) | Credit lowers the contract; placed on the draft final invoice as a negative line, or on a draft credit memo once the final invoice was sent; waits when there's no final invoice yet. The on-time credit shows "Credit applied". Event `credit.updated`.                  |
| Job reaches **Completed**                                | The final invoice is drafted (contract − everything billed, approved credits as lines) by `ensureFinalInvoice()`; Needs attention says to send it.                                                                                                                        |
| Invoice **sent**                                         | PDF stored in Documents, simulated email (PDF attached) and/or text, timeline entry, event `invoice.sent`, accounting preview; a sent final invoice moves Completed → **Invoiced** through the gate.                                                                      |
| Payment brings the balance to $0                         | Invoice statuses refresh; Invoiced → **Paid in Full** through `advanceIfGatePasses()`; event `payment.created`.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Payment **bounced**                                      | Payment stops counting; invoices it paid reopen; timeline entry; event `payment.bounced` → finance notified. A job already Paid in Full is not moved back; Needs attention shows "Paid stage with a balance".                                                             |
| Refund                                                   | Comes off unapplied money first, then the newest invoices; invoice statuses refresh; event `refund.created`.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Worker every minute (`sweepMoney`)                       | `sweep_invoices` refreshes overdue status and emits `invoice.overdue` once per invoice at **7** and **30** days past due → one simulated reminder to the customer (text, else email; draft wording) and a finance notification. Money gates re-checked for invoiced jobs. |
| Attention engine (every minute)                          | Overdue invoice (Medium at 7 days, High at 30; editable), Sold no deposit (deposit invoice not paid N days after the sale).                                                                                                                                               |
| Month closed / reopened (Admin → Finance)                | Summary snapshot saved; records dated in the month locked; event `month.closed` (reopen: `month_close.updated`).                                                                                                                                                          |

_Phase 8:_ the overdue reminders now go out through workflow 14 (the sweep still raises `invoice.overdue` once per threshold); deposit release, invoicing and the Paid in Full move stay built in.
